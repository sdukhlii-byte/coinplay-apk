CoinPlay App FAQ


Which cryptocurrencies can I deposit with?

CoinPlay accepts 40+ cryptocurrencies, including Bitcoin, Ethereum, Litecoin, Bitcoin Cash, USDT on several blockchains, meme coins such as DOGE and SHIB, and fan tokens of football clubs. The exact list is shown in the cashier inside your account and may change.

How do I deposit with crypto?

Open the cashier, choose a coin and network, copy the deposit address or scan the QR code, and send the amount from your wallet or exchange. Deposits are usually credited within minutes, after the network confirms the transaction.

How fast are withdrawals?

Withdrawals usually take a few minutes. The exact speed and fee depend on the blockchain network you choose.

Can I hold several coins in one account?

Yes. You can create multiple cryptocurrency accounts and switch between coins while you play.

Any tips for a safe crypto deposit?

Send the same coin on the same network that you selected in the cashier, use only the address shown there, and start with a small test amount if you are unsure. Blockchain transactions cannot be reversed. Turn on two-factor authentication in your account settings.

How do I sign up?

Create an account with your email, or with your Google or Facebook account. It usually takes a few minutes. Then make a deposit and start playing.

Is the app safe to install?

Install it only from this site or the official CoinPlay website. Android shows a standard warning for any app installed outside Google Play.

Is there a CoinPlay app for iPhone?

On iPhone you can play in Safari and add CoinPlay to your home screen for one-tap access. The CoinPlay website may also offer an iOS app link at the bottom of its pages.

Why does Android show a warning when I install the APK?

Android shows this for every app installed outside Google Play. It is a standard safety prompt and not specific to CoinPlay.

Do I need a new account for the app?

No. Use the same login as on the website.

How do I update the app?

Download the latest APK from this site and install it over the old one. Your account and balance stay the same.

Is support available?

Yes, live chat support is available 24/7 from any page of the CoinPlay website.

Where is CoinPlay not available?

CoinPlay is not offered in the USA, the UK, Slovakia, France, the Netherlands and their territories, including Curaçao. Always check the gambling laws in your own country.

The download does not start. What should I do?

Try another browser, check that you have enough free space and a stable connection. You can also play in the browser or via Telegram.
