Quick FAQ

Which cryptocurrencies can I deposit with?

CoinPlay accepts 40+ cryptocurrencies, including Bitcoin, Ethereum, Litecoin, Bitcoin Cash, USDT on several blockchains, meme coins such as DOGE and SHIB, and fan tokens of football clubs. The exact list is shown in the cashier inside your account and may change.

How do I deposit with crypto?

Open the cashier, choose a coin and network, copy the deposit address or scan the QR code, and send the amount from your wallet or exchange. Deposits are usually credited within minutes, after the network confirms the transaction.

How fast are withdrawals?

Withdrawals usually take a few minutes. The exact speed and fee depend on the blockchain network you choose.

Can I hold several coins in one account?

Yes. You can create multiple cryptocurrency accounts and switch between coins while you play.

Any tips for a safe crypto deposit?

Send the same coin on the same network that you selected in the cashier, use only the address shown there, and start with a small test amount if you are unsure. Blockchain transactions cannot be reversed. Turn on two-factor authentication in your account settings.
