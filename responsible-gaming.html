Responsible gaming

Gambling should be entertainment, not a way to make money. Play responsibly.

Tips

  • Set a deposit and time limit before you start and stick to it.
  • Only play with money you can afford to lose.
  • Do not chase losses.
  • Take regular breaks.
  • Never play under the influence of alcohol or when upset.

Signs of a problem

  • Spending more time or money than planned
  • Borrowing money to gamble
  • Hiding gambling from family or friends

Where to get help

If gambling is causing problems, talk to someone you trust and contact a local support organization in your country. You can also ask CoinPlay support to set limits or close your account.
